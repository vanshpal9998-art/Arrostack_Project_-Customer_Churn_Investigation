# Customer Churn Investigation
## Business Questions and Analytical Scope

### 1. Stakeholder

**Primary Stakeholder:** Customer Retention Manager

The retention manager wants to understand why customers leave
the telecommunications company and identify opportunities
to improve customer retention.

### 2. Business Problem

Customer churn affects company revenue and long-term growth.

The company needs to understand customer behaviour, identify
high-risk customer groups, and develop effective retention
strategies.

### 3. Project Objectives

1. Measure the overall customer churn rate.
2. Identify customer characteristics associated with churn.
3. Compare churn rates across contract types.
4. Understand the relationship between tenure and churn.
5. Investigate payment methods and customer churn.
6. Identify customer segments using K-Means clustering.
7. Recommend practical customer retention strategies.

### 4. Key Business Questions

**Question 1:** What percentage of customers have left the company?

**Question 2:** Which contract type has the highest churn rate?

**Question 3:** Are customers with shorter tenure more likely to leave?

**Question 4:** Which payment method is associated with the highest churn rate?

**Question 5:** Do customers with higher monthly charges show different churn patterns?

**Question 6:** Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?

**Question 7:** What retention strategies could help reduce customer churn?

### 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | Total number of customer records |
| Churned Customers | Number of customers who left |
| Retained Customers | Number of customers who stayed |
| Churn Rate | Percentage of customers who left |
| Average Tenure | Average customer subscription duration |
| Average Monthly Charges | Average monthly customer charges |
| Segment Churn Rate | Churn percentage within each cluster |

### 6. Churn Rate Formula

Churn Rate = (Churned Customers / Total Customers) × 100

### 7. Project Scope

**Included:**
- Data understanding
- Data cleaning
- Data quality validation
- Exploratory data analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Business insights
- Retention recommendations

**Not included:**
- Live customer data integration
- Production deployment
- Real-time churn monitoring
- Causal analysis of customer churn

### 8. Expected Business Outcomes

- Better understanding of customer churn patterns.
- Identification of high-risk customer groups.
- Improved customer retention planning.
- Evidence-based business recommendations.

### 9. Analytical Limitations

This project uses historical observational data.
Associations between customer characteristics and churn
do not establish causation.

Retention strategies should be tested and evaluated
before large-scale implementation.
